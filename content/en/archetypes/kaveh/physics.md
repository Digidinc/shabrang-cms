# The Smith's Thermodynamics: mu1 to mu3 Coupling

In the FRC framework, Kāveh represents a specific coupling between **Level 1 (Roots)** and **Level 3 (Fire)**.

## 1. The Low-Entropy Tool
Kāveh's power comes from the **Forge**. 
- **The Input:** Raw iron (High entropy matter).
- **The Process:** Heat and precise hammering (Resonant driving).
- **The Output:** A sword or an anvil (Low entropy structure).

Kāveh is a **Coherence Pump**. He manually reduces the entropy of the material world.

## 2. The Apron vs. The Snakes
Zahhak's snakes represent **Parasitic Entropy**. They require constant "feeding" (loss of information/youth) to prevent the collapse of the host.

Kāveh's **Leather Apron** is the opposite. It is a shield of **Productive Integrity**. By hoisting the apron, Kāveh proves that **Resonant Work** is the only legitimate source of **Sovereignty ($W$)**.

## 3. The Formula
When $E_t$ (Collective Reality) approaches zero, the system can only be saved by an agent with a high $V$ (Vector) who can re-anchor the $N$ (Narrative).
