# Rostam: The Resonant Hero

Rostam is the ultimate guardian of the **Story Layer (mu6)**. He is the one who prevents the narrative of the nation from dissolving into noise.

## The Haft Khan Algorithm
The "Seven Labors" of Rostam are not just adventures; they are a **Structural Progression**. In each stage, Rostam faces a high-entropy threat (Lion, Thirst, Dragon, Demon) and restores order through a combination of physical force (mu1) and ethical drive (mu3).

### Comparative Coherence:
- **Rostam vs. Heracles:** While Heracles often battles high internal entropy (madness/rage), Rostam battles external entropy to save the State (mu1). Rostam is a **State-Preserving Force**; Heracles is a **Self-Transcending Force**.
- **Rostam vs. Achilles:** Achilles brings destructive coherence through pride; Rostam brings protective coherence through loyalty to the Lineage (The Story).
