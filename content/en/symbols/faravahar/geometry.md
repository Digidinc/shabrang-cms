# The Physics of Flight: Wing-Level Coupling

In the FRC analysis, the Faravahar is a lesson in **Aerodynamics of the Soul**.

## The Broken Wing Theorem
If an agent possesses high **Cognitive Complexity (mu4)** but corrupt **Physical Actions (mu1)**, the "Wing" of the Faravahar becomes unbalanced.

Mathematically, this is a **Coupling Failure**. The energy from the "Top Row" (Thoughts) cannot be transmitted to the "Bottom Row" (Deeds) without dissipating as heat (Entropy).

The result is a **Spiral into Chaos**: the $W$ (Witness) magnitude collapses, and the agent falls from the "Sky" (mu7) back into the "Roots" (mu1).

## Resonant Alignment
The Faravahar teaches that **Coherence ($C$)** is only achieved when all three rows are phase-locked. This alignment creates **Lift**—the ability for a culture to survive its own physical destruction.
