# The Linguistic Ark: The Nursery Protocol

How do you save a language when the state, the schools, and the courts have been conquered? You move it to the only place an invader cannot reach: the **Nursery (mu2)**.

## 1. The Emotional Firewall
The Persian mothers of the 7th and 8th centuries understood a fundamental rule of **Information Theory**: Signal preservation requires high-intensity emotional grounding ($V$).

While the public world shifted to Arabic for administration (mu4), the domestic world remained Persian. By singing lullabies and telling stories in the mother tongue, they ensured that the **Language of Emotion** remained Iranian.

## 2. The Incubation Period
For 200 years, the Persian mind was in a state of **Linguistic Kenosis** (self-emptying). It allowed the external "Hardware" to be replaced while the internal "Software" was silently updated. 

This incubation allowed the language to simplify, losing complex Pahlavi grammar and becoming the streamlined, resonant "New Persian" that would eventually power the *Shahnameh*.

## 3. The Result: Resonant Explosion
When the "Silence" ended in the 9th century, the language didn't just return; it exploded. Because it had been preserved in the **Rhythm (mu2)** and **Fire (mu3)** layers, it possessed a lyrical intensity that Arabic—at that time a language of law and logic—could not match.
