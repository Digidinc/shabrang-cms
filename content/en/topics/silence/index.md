# The Two Centuries of Silence: 650–850 AD

A study in **Linguistic Kenosis**.

## The Apparent Death
After the fall of the Sasanians, the "Hardware" of the Persian state was dismantled. Arabic became the language of the public square. To the world, the Persian mind had been deleted.

## The Hidden Incubation
But the "Signal" migrated to the kitchen and the cradle. 
The **Nursery Protocol** ensured that while children learned Arabic for their careers (mu4), they received their soul in Persian (mu2).
