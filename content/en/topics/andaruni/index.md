# Andaruni: The Hidden Core

Andaruni is not just a room in a house; it is a **Topological Defense**.

## The Coherence Shield
In a high-entropy environment, energy dissipates quickly. If you leave a hot cup of tea in the snow, it cools. If you leave a culture in the middle of a war zone, it fragments.

Andaruni creates a **Boundary Condition**. By "hiding" the core values, the civilization reduces its interaction with the external noise, allowing the internal resonance to remain high ($C \approx 1$).
