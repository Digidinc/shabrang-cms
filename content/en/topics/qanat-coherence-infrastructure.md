# The Qanat: Infrastructure of Hidden Coherence

To survive in the desert, you must hide your life.

The Iranian plateau is a high-entropy environment. The sun is relentless; the evaporation rate is lethal. A river on the surface dies.

The **Qanat** is the engineering solution to this thermodynamic problem. It is a subterranean aqueduct that taps into the water table of the mountains and carries it, often for kilometers, underground to the arid plains.

## The Logic of Andaruni

This physical necessity shaped the Persian metaphysical mind. It taught the civilization that:

1.  **Surface appearances are deceptive.** The ground looks dry, but life flows beneath.
2.  **Survival depends on depth.** Shallow roots die; deep channels live.
3.  **Coherence requires protection.** The valuable thing (water, truth, family, faith) must be shielded from the "noise" of the public sphere.

This is the origin of **Andaruni** (The Inner). The Persian home, with its high walls and central courtyard, is a Qanat for the soul. It creates a low-entropy zone where the culture can breathe, protected from the high-entropy state outside.

In FRC terms, the Qanat is a **Coherence Shield**. It allows the system to maintain high internal order ($C$) even when the external environment ($\Omega$) is chaotic.
