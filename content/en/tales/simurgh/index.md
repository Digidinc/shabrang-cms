# The Simurgh: The Architecture of Unity

The story of the Simurgh is the operating manual for **mu7 (The Sky)**.

## The Seven Valleys as mu-Stack
The birds travel through seven valleys, which map directly to the **Ascent through the mu-Levels**:

1.  **Quest (mu1):** Physical beginning.
2.  **Love (mu3):** The ignition of the Fire.
3.  **Knowledge (mu4):** Mapping the path.
4.  **Detachment (mu5):** Moving into the Garden.
5.  **Unity (mu7):** Achieving the Sky.

## The Si-Morgh Paradox
When thirty (*Si*) birds (*Morgh*) arrive, they look into the mirror and see only themselves. They realized that **Coherence is the King**. The "Simurgh" is not a bird; it is the **Resonant State** of the thirty birds.

This proves the FRC thesis: **Identity is a Field**, not a node.
