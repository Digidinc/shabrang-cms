# The Whirling Pumpkin (Kodu-ye Ghelgheleh-zan)

A story of how the weak outsmart the strong using the power of the "Linguistic Ark."

## The Story
An old woman wants to visit her daughter. On the way, she encounters a wolf, a tiger, and a lion, all wanting to eat her. She promises them she will be much fatter on her return trip. On her way back, she hides inside a large, hollowed-out pumpkin and rolls past the predators while reciting a rhythmic rhyme:

*"Ghelghele-zan, Kodu-ye man! (Roll away, my pumpkin!)"*

## FRC Analysis

### mu2 (Rhythm): The Pulse of Survival
The old woman doesn't use physical force. She uses a repetitive, rhythmic sound-form to distract and bypass the high-entropy threat (the predators). The rhyme creates a state of "Phase-Locking" that allows her to navigate through the danger.

### mu1 (Roots): The Hollow Fortress
The pumpkin itself is a physical manifestation of the "Hollow Fortress." It is a hard, protective exterior that contains the "flowing" source of life (the grandmother).

### The Linguistic Ark
By encoding her strategy into a catchy nursery rhyme, the grandmother ensures that the "Protocol of Survival" can be remembered and passed down to children. This is how the culture survives when the walls are broken.
